## 🎯 Dashboard Goals

* **Optimise Staffing Schedules**: Align barista staffing levels with peak customer transaction hours.
* **Maximise Menu Profitability**: Identify top-performing beverages and evaluate underperforming menu items.
* **Track Regional Performance**: Monitor branch sales performance across UK geographic locations.
* **Evaluate Shift Revenue**: Analyse revenue distribution across morning, afternoon, and night operational shifts.

---

## 📊 Chart Explanations & Analytical Insights

### 1. Executive KPI Cards
* Displays overall business performance metrics including Total Revenue (£112.2k+), Total Orders (3,547), and Average Order Value (£31.65).
* Gives executives instant visibility into top-line retail sales growth and transaction volumes.

### 2. Peak Rush Hour Heatmap (Chart 1)
* Maps hourly transaction density across weekdays using an intuitive colour-coded grid.
* Pinpoints peak morning and afternoon traffic hours to streamline staff scheduling and queue management.

### 3. Menu Performance Matrix (Chart 2)
* Ranks beverage sales volume to highlight core revenue drivers such as Lattes and Americanos.
* Enables targeted menu engineering, promotional bundling, and upselling strategies.

### 4. Sales Trend Line Chart (Chart 3)
* Tracks daily revenue fluctuations over time to identify sales seasonality and trajectory.
* Provides a baseline for demand forecasting and inventory management.

### 5. Shift Share Breakdown (Chart 4)
* Displays revenue distribution across Morning (32%), Afternoon (34%), and Night (34%) shifts.
* Helps operational managers balance labour costs against shift revenue generation.

### 6. UK Branch Location Map (Chart 5)
* Visualises regional performance across UK postcodes using customised geographic boundaries.
* Identifies high-performing retail clusters and potential geographic expansion targets.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Filters

* **Time of Day Filter**: Allows users to isolate metrics by Morning, Afternoon, or Night operational windows.
* **Coffee Name Filter**: Enables granular analysis of individual beverage product performance.
* **Date Range Filter**: Filters the entire dashboard dynamically across specific custom date ranges.
* **Cross-Chart Actions**: Clicking any chart segment or map location instantly filters all accompanying views on the dashboard.
